Cleaner processes all file scans, duplicates identification, and storage calculations strictly locally on your device. We never upload, transmit, monitor, or store your private photos, videos, or documents on external cloud servers.

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Mecorven ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information in connection with the Cleaner mobile application for Android. By installing and using Cleaner, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

Our principal mission is to provide powerful, transparent device storage optimization without compromising your digital privacy.

02 Data We Access & Permissions Required

To function as a device cleanup and media management tool, Cleaner requires access to device storage. Here is an explicit breakdown of what permissions we request and why:

Permission Purpose Processing Type
Read Media Images & Videos
(READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO)		 To scan for duplicate photos, large video files, similar images, and screenshots you may choose to remove. 100% Local (On-Device)
All Files Access
(MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)		 Used strictly for core cleaning features: locating temporary cache files, obsolete APKs, large leftover downloads, and WhatsApp media cleanup selected by the user. 100% Local (On-Device)
Storage Stats & Filesystem
(StatFs / Environment)		 Calculates real-time internal storage statistics (total capacity, used gigabytes, and free disk space) displayed on the home dashboard. 100% Local (On-Device)

Explicit Exclusion: We do not request or utilize QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES. We do not inspect personal installed applications, contacts, location, microphone, or camera data.

03 How We Use Scanned Data

Information accessed on your device is used solely to execute user-initiated actions:

  • Duplicate & Similar Photo Detection: Comparing visual and structural hashes of media locally to present cleaning recommendations.
  • Large Files & WhatsApp Media: Identifying high-capacity media files so you can reclaim device storage.
  • Cache & Junk File Removal: Deleting unneeded temporary files only after you review and approve the action.

04 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We believe your personal files belong only to you:

  • No Data Sales: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal files, media, or usage information.
  • No File Uploads: Files selected for cleanup are deleted directly through Android's local filesystem APIs. No file content ever leaves your device.
  • Diagnostic Services: Standard, non-personally identifiable diagnostic or crash reports may be collected by Google Play Services solely to ensure app stability.

05 User Control & Managing Permissions

You have full authority over all permissions:

  • You can grant or revoke storage permissions at any time via Settings > Apps > Cleaner > Permissions.
  • Revoking storage access disables scanning features but does not alter or delete any existing files on your phone.
  • All file deletions within the app require explicit user confirmation.

06 Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Cleaner does not knowingly solicit or collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any minor has provided personal data, please contact us and we will promptly address it.
